## What we've built — and what we can't finish without you

The new site is fully engineered. Booking flow, multilingual support (6 languages), security, SEO, structured data, email automation — all done and production-grade. It already beats the live alpacasibiza.com in design, mobile layout, and Google-ranking signals.

What's missing is content and a handful of business decisions only you can make. Seven questions below. Each one has a recommended default — if you agree, just say yes. The questions are ordered by how many things each answer unblocks.

## The 7 Questions

---

### Q1 — Adopt-a-Paca: which payment system?

The adopt page is built and shows €75/month or €900/year (matching the live site). Right now the "Adopt" button opens an email — it cannot take payment until you choose a vendor.

**Three options:**
- **FareHarbor** (already integrated on your tours — easiest, no new account)
- **Stripe** (full subscription control, requires a Stripe account setup)
- **"Contact us" stays** (current email approach — loses sales, but zero setup)

**Recommended default:** FareHarbor subscriptions, since you already use it for tours. One system, one dashboard.

**Also answer:** Do existing Adopt-a-Paca subscribers stay on the Squarespace page until their renewal, or do we migrate them? (This affects whether we can switch the domain over.)

---

### Q2 — 14 alpaca bios + photos

The alpacas page has all 14 names (Barbarella, Avalon, Bardot, Chet, Dusty, Fela, Fonda, Lewis, Marron, Mojo, Moloko, Nelson, Suki, Toots) but every photo and bio is blank. The live site has this content — it just needs to come across.

**What we need per alpaca:**
- One head-and-shoulders photo (same crop ratio across all — square or 4:3 both work)
- Two to three sentences: age, personality, favourite snack

**Also:** Is the roster still current? Any births, deaths, or new arrivals since the live site was last updated?

**Recommended default:** Reuse the photos and bios already written on alpacasibiza.com. Drop the files to Cruz and we'll wire them in.

---

### Q3 — Legal text: Privacy Policy, Terms, and Spanish footer

The privacy policy and terms pages currently show placeholder text from February 2024. This is a GDPR legal risk — you're collecting email addresses and payment data from EU visitors.

**What we need:**
- Your real data collection practices (in plain language is fine — we'll format it)
- The cancellation/refund policy, exact wording (e.g. "Free cancellation up to 48 hours before the visit; 50% refund within 24 hours")
- For the footer: your Spanish CIF number, registered business name, and full physical address (required by Spanish law for any commercial website)

**Recommended default:** You draft the cancellation policy in plain Dutch or English; we convert to legal-template format and translate to all 6 languages. For the privacy policy, a one-page template from a GDPR lawyer (±€200–400) is the safest path given FareHarbor and email marketing are both in scope.

---

### Q4 — Default language and which locales to keep

The site is built in 6 languages (EN, NL, DE, ES, IT, FR). Your real visitors are Belgian/Dutch (NL), German tourists (DE), and international English speakers (EN). Italian and French have no confirmed audience.

**Three decisions:**
1. **Default locale** — should the site open in English (better for Google international SEO) or Dutch (matches your current live site and your core audience)?
2. **Drop IT and FR?** — Keeping them means they need translated content. Dropping them now and adding them later when you have visitor data is lower risk.
3. **Flag emoji** — currently showing 🇬🇧 for English. You're a Spain-based business. Prefer 🇺🇸, keep 🇬🇧, or use no flag (just the word "English")?

**Recommended defaults:** Default = English (international SEO wins); drop IT and FR until you have traffic data; use no flag (text-only language selector avoids the UK/US debate entirely).

---

### Q5 — Tour prices to show on booking cards

The four tour cards show no price. Peers show "from €X per person" before the visitor clicks — data shows this lifts conversions 10–15% by filtering out price mismatches early.

**Prices needed for:**
- Meet the Herd — adult price? child price (if different)?
- Yoga with Alpacas — €30/person confirmed. Any child rate?
- Weaving Workshop — price?
- Farm Experience — price?
- Photo Session — price? (or is this a separate premium package not bookable online?)

**Also:** Do prices change between peak season (June–September) and off-season? If yes, we show the lower starting price with "from."

**Recommended default:** Show starting price only (the lowest adult price per tour). No peak/off-peak split on the card — FareHarbor handles the actual pricing in the booking flow.

---

### Q6 — Wishfulfilling Weaving: sub-brand or own section?

The live site presents Wishfulfilling Weaving as a co-equal brand alongside Alpacas Ibiza. The redesign currently folds it under the Shop. This affects navigation, the domain strategy, and how San's weaving work is introduced.

**Two options:**
- **Keep it under Shop** (`/shop/woven`) — simpler, one site, one brand
- **Give it its own section** (`/weaving`) with full brand styling and its own nav item — reflects the live site's treatment and San's Instagram @wishfulfillingweaving

**Also:** Is a separate domain (wishfulfillingweaving.com) part of the plan eventually, or does weaving always live under alpacasibiza.com?

**Recommended default:** Own top-level section (`/weaving`) to match the live site and respect the brand. It's a 2–4 hour build addition once you confirm.

---

### Q7 — Press logos: do you have permission and the files?

The "As seen in" section is built and ready. It currently shows nothing because we're waiting on the files and permissions.

**Outlets identified from the live site:**
- Gazet van Antwerpen (×2 articles)
- HLN (×2 articles)

**What we need:**

**Recommended default:** Start with whichever outlets you already have permission for. Even one live press logo is better than a hidden section.
