What Your New Website Is

Your new website is a modern, conversion-focused home for Ibiza's first alpaca farm and weaving studio. It replaces the current site with a faster, more welcoming experience built around four things visitors want to do: book a tour, shop handwoven pieces, request a custom commission, and buy Alcaca (alpaca manure for the garden). The site handles six languages, connects directly to your FareHarbor booking calendar so guests can reserve their spot without calling or emailing, and sends automatic confirmation and reminder emails through your Resend account — all without you touching a button. It is fully built; what remains is plugging in your content, your photos, and a handful of account keys before it goes live. (Source: README.md, START_HERE.md)

Privacy & Legal — Action Required Before EU Traffic

The site currently has Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Cookie Policy pages — but the text on all three is generic placeholder copy. Publishing them as-is to real visitors carries legal risk under GDPR, because the pages do not describe your actual data practices (what FareHarbor collects, what Resend stores, what GA4 tracks, or who to contact for data removal requests).

Before the site goes live to any EU visitors: replace the placeholder text on all three legal pages with language that reflects your real data handling. At minimum this covers: what data you collect and why, who processes it (FareHarbor, Resend, Google Analytics, Cloudflare), how long you keep it, your contact details as data controller, and your cancellation/refund terms. A lawyer review is recommended for an EU-registered business. (Source: OWNER_INPUT_NEEDED.md — Privacy Policy & Terms of Service; README.md — Legal & Compliance)
